# EigenQuest

**Exploring AI and Machine Learning through experiments, implementation, and curiosity.**

EigenQuest is my personal AI/ML learning lab. It's where I explore how intelligent systems learn, make decisions, recognize patterns, and adapt through experimentation.

Rather than simply following tutorials or copying implementations, I aim to understand the underlying concepts, build things myself, test ideas, and learn from failures.

## What I'm Exploring

- **Machine Learning** — learning algorithms, prediction, and pattern recognition.
- **Neural Networks** — understanding how models learn, from fundamentals to deeper architectures.
- **Reinforcement Learning** — training agents through interaction, rewards, and trial and error.
- **Multi-Agent Systems** — exploring how agents compete, cooperate, and adapt to each other.
- **Computer Vision** — teaching computers to interpret images and live camera feeds.
- **AI Experiments** — testing unusual ideas and discovering what intelligent systems can do.

## My Approach

1. Start with a question or an idea.
2. Learn the concepts needed to investigate it.
3. Implement and experiment rather than blindly copying.
4. Test results, investigate failures, and understand limitations.
5. Document what I learn and explore what comes next.

## Experiments

This repository will grow organically as I explore different areas of AI and ML. Experiments may range from small implementations of fundamental algorithms to agents that learn to play games or interact with simulated environments.

Some experiments will succeed. Others will fail. Both are part of the learning process.

## Goal

To develop a practical and fundamental understanding of AI and machine learning by building, experimenting, questioning, and continuously learning.

**Not just using AI. Understanding how it works.**
